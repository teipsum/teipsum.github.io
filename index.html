The problem

The industry settled how agents talk. It skipped how they answer for anything.

Agent platforms can route a task across teams, vendors and organizations. None of them can tell you, afterward, what the agent was permitted to do, which facts it acted on, or who stood behind the decision. No terms. No receipts. No provenance.

In regulated industries that isn't a gap in convenience. It's the reason the deployment never leaves the pilot.

01

Governed identity

Every agent acts under an enforced identity with explicit scope and authority. Permission is a property of the call, not a promise in a policy document.

02

Signed records

Every act produces a signed, replayable record: what was used, who certified it, under which authority, at what time. Verifiable rather than asserted.

03

Your perimeter

Runs where your data already lives. When your rules change they take effect on your decision, not on a vendor's release cycle. And work you can hold accountable is work you can put under contract, across companies.

The proof
